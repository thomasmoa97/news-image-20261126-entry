A make.fun news-image entry: one image summing up the world news of
Thursday 26 November 2026, drawn as a newspaper front page.

**The stories** (each verified against reporting dated 26 November 2026):

- **Hero — North Korea:** Kim Jong Un orders wider deployment of tactical
  nuclear weapons after touring nuclear-material sites; KCNA calls the
  arsenal "absolutely inviolable".
- **USA:** A nor'easter slams the East Coast on Thanksgiving Day; the 100th
  Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade is postponed to Saturday.
- **Middle East:** Saudi defences intercept Houthi ballistic missiles aimed
  at Riyadh overnight; a projectile hits Jazan port and a drone is downed
  near Jeddah.
- **Markets:** Brent crude tops $94 a barrel as the Red Sea attacks spread.
- **Australia:** Private business investment jumps 12.2% in Q3, the biggest
  quarterly rise since 2011.
- **Japan:** Bank of Japan board member Tamura signals faster interest-rate
  hikes if inflation overshoots.

**How it works:** `draw_image.py` (Pillow, run locally) draws `entry.png`
(1024×1024). `main.py` is standard library only — it copies the committed
`entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` after verifying its SHA-256 digest
before and after the copy, so both grader runs produce identical bytes.
